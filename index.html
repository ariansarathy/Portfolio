# About me

I'm a Computer Science student at Montclair State University who likes building software that's actually useful to people — from AI assistants that answer thousands of student questions to tools that help people focus.

I've worked across generative AI (RAG pipelines, LangChain, vector search), cloud data engineering (AWS Lambda, S3, Glue), and full-stack development (React, Next.js, Node.js, Flask). Right now I'm co-founding TideTrack, a Chrome extension for tracking focused study time, and supporting students and faculty on Montclair State's IT service desk.

In 2025 I wrote and published The Amazing World of Python: Youth Coders Edition, a beginner's book that walks young readers from their first print() to AI concepts.

Outside of code I'm involved in DECA, the Finance Society, and the Robotics Club, and I've been a youth ambassador for the Akshaya Patra Foundation since 2023, helping fundraise to fight childhood hunger (leadership.md).
